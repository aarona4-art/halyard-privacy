Delete your Halyard data

This page explains how to ask for your data to be deleted from the Halyard: Half-Staff Alerts app (com.halyard.flagstatus). Halyard has no accounts, so there is no account to close.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@halyardflagstatus.com with the subject "Delete my Halyard data".
  2. Tell us your device model and roughly when you installed the app, so we can find your records. We do not collect your name or email, so we may ask for a little more detail.
  3. We will delete your data and reply to confirm, within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your push notification token and device platform.
  • Your alert settings, including any followed states.

What is kept

  • Your request email, kept only as long as needed to handle it and to show we responded.
  • Data held by Google (including AdMob and Google Play), Apple and RevenueCat is governed by their own policies. You can manage your advertising ID in your device settings, and you can ask RevenueCat to delete purchase records through us.

Stop alerts right away

Uninstalling the app or turning off notifications in your device settings stops alerts immediately.

See also the Halyard Privacy Policy.
