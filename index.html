Halyard Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Halyard ("we", "us") makes the Halyard mobile app, which sends alerts when the U.S. flag is ordered to half-staff. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. Questions can be sent to support@halyardflagstatus.com.

1. Summary

  • There are no accounts. You never give us your name, email address, or phone number.
  • To send alerts, we store a push notification token for your device and your alert choices.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects its own data as described below.
  • Subscriptions are processed by Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. We never see your payment card details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information we collect and use

Information stored on our server

  • Push notification token and device platform. Used to deliver the alerts you ask for.
  • Your alert settings. This includes which notifications are turned on and, for Premium subscribers, which states you follow.

Information collected by our service providers

  • Google AdMob (free version only). To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your advertising ID, IP address (from which approximate location can be inferred), ad interactions such as taps and impressions, app launches, and diagnostic information about the ad SDK.
  • RevenueCat. To manage the Premium subscription, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase and subscription status from Google Play or the App Store.
  • Google Play / Apple. Payments are handled entirely by the store. We receive confirmation that a subscription is active, not your payment details.

Premium subscription

Halyard Premium is an optional annual subscription. It renews automatically every year until you cancel, at the price shown in the app before you subscribe. Payment is processed by Google Play, or by Apple on iOS, and we never see or store your payment details. You can cancel or manage your subscription at any time in the Google Play Store (Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions) or in your Apple ID subscription settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep Premium until the end of the period you have already paid for.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, files, messages, health data, or browsing history.

3. How we use information

  • To send the half-staff alerts you have enabled.
  • To show the state-specific status you follow as a Premium subscriber.
  • To confirm your Premium subscription and remove ads.
  • To show ads in the free version and to keep the service secure and working.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help run the app, and only as needed for the purposes above:

  • Expo (push notification service) and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, to deliver alerts to your device.
  • Railway, which hosts our server and database.
  • Google AdMob, to serve ads in the free version.
  • RevenueCat, to manage subscriptions.

We may also disclose information if required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Advertising and your choices

  • Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, and some U.S. states), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent tools. You can change that choice later from the app's settings where offered.
  • On iOS, the app asks permission before tracking across other companies' apps and websites, and respects your answer.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Settings, then Google, then Ads).
  • Premium removes ads from the app.
  • You can turn off notifications at any time in the app or in your device settings.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your push token and alert settings for as long as needed to provide alerts. You can ask us to delete them at any time by emailing support@halyardflagstatus.com. Uninstalling the app stops alerts to your device. Data held by Google, Apple, and RevenueCat is governed by their own policies and retention periods.

7. Security

Data sent between the app and our server is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Halyard is intended for people 18 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, email support@halyardflagstatus.com. Because we do not collect your name or email, we may ask for details about your device to find your records.

10. Not a government service

Halyard is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any government agency. Half-staff information comes from public proclamations and may be delayed or incomplete, so confirm with official sources before acting on it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version at this address and update the effective date above.
